## Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Aurexus Group Ltd ("Aurexus", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on [https://www.aurexus-group.com](https://www.aurexus-group.com).

It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

**Effective date:** 30 July 2026  
**Last updated:** 30 July 2026  

## What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage. In this policy we refer to these collectively as "cookies" unless we need to be more specific.

## Current Use on This Website

The corporate website is intentionally lightweight. As of the last updated date:

- We **do not** load third-party analytics cookies (for example Google Analytics)
- We **do not** load advertising or marketing pixels
- We **do not** embed social media plugins that set third-party cookies
- We **do** use local browser storage for strictly necessary preferences described below

If we introduce non-essential analytics or marketing technologies in future, they will only run after you give consent through our cookie preferences controls, and this policy will be updated.

## Categories We Use

### Strictly necessary

Required for the website to operate and for us to remember your privacy choices.

| Name / storage key | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `aurexus_cookie_consent_v1` | Local storage | Stores your cookie preference choices so we can honour them | Until cleared or updated by you |
| Theme preference (`theme` / next-themes storage) | Local storage | Remembers dark or light mode | Until cleared or updated by you |

**Lawful basis:** Necessary for service delivery / legitimate interests. Consent is not required for strictly necessary storage under UK PECR where it is essential.

### Analytics and performance

**Not currently used** on this corporate website.

If enabled in future, these would help us understand aggregated traffic and page performance, and would require your consent before loading.

### Marketing

**Not currently used** on this corporate website.

If enabled in future, these would require your consent before loading.

## Hosting and Security Logs

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request logs (such as IP address, user agent and requested URL) to operate and secure the website. These are not browser cookies set by Aurexus marketing tools; they are operational logs processed as described in the Privacy Policy.

## Managing Your Preferences

When you first visit, you can:

- **Accept non-essential cookies** (currently none are loaded; your choice is stored for future use)
- **Reject non-essential cookies**
- **Manage preferences** by category

You can reopen preferences at any time using the **Cookie settings** control in the website footer.

You can also clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may reset theme and consent preferences.

## How Consent Controls Work

Non-essential scripts and tags are gated behind your stored consent preference. They will not load unless the relevant category is enabled. Because analytics and marketing are not currently deployed, rejecting non-essential cookies has no additional script impact today, but ensures those categories remain off if introduced later before you update preferences.

## Personal Data

Information stored through preferences may constitute personal data when combined with other data. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and [GDPR Information](/legal/gdpr).
