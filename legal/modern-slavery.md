## Statement

This statement is made pursuant to section 54(1) of the Modern Slavery Act 2015. It sets out the steps Aurexus Group Ltd ("Aurexus", "we", "us" or "our") has taken and continues to take to understand and address modern slavery and human trafficking risks in our business and supply chains.

## Organisational Context

Aurexus is an early-stage, growing UK private limited company. Our operations are primarily based in the United Kingdom, focused on technology development, applied research and professional services. We have a small team and a supply chain consisting mainly of:

- Cloud infrastructure and software-as-a-service providers
- Professional services (legal, accounting, advisory)
- Hardware and office suppliers
- Subcontractors and freelance specialists in software engineering and research

Given our sector, scale and UK-centred operations, we assess our direct exposure to modern slavery risk as relatively low. However, we recognise that modern slavery can exist in any supply chain — including technology hardware, facilities services and international subcontracting — and we take our responsibilities seriously.

## Our Commitment

We have a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery and human trafficking. We are committed to:

- Acting ethically and with integrity in all business dealings
- Implementing effective systems and controls to safeguard against slavery and trafficking in our operations and supply chains
- Ensuring transparency and proportionate due diligence as we grow
- Encouraging reporting of concerns without fear of retaliation

## Due Diligence

As a growing organisation, our due diligence is proportionate to our current scale:

### Operations

- We comply with UK employment law, including National Minimum Wage, working time and right-to-work requirements
- Employment contracts and engagement terms are documented
- We do not use forced, bonded or involuntary labour

### Supply chain

- We prefer established suppliers with published ethical and compliance commitments
- We assess higher-risk suppliers (those involving international manufacturing, low-cost labour-intensive services or complex subcontracting) with additional scrutiny as engagements arise
- Contractual terms require compliance with the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and applicable labour laws

We will formalise a supplier due diligence checklist and risk assessment process as our procurement activity increases.

## Risk Assessment

We consider the following risk factors:

| Area | Risk level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Direct employment (UK) | Low | UK employment law compliance, fair terms |
| Cloud/SaaS providers | Low | Reputable providers with published policies |
| Hardware procurement | Medium (potential) | Source from reputable vendors; assess as volume grows |
| International subcontractors | Medium (potential) | Contractual compliance, selective engagement |
| Office and facilities services | Low | UK-based suppliers |

We will reassess risks annually and when entering new markets or supply relationships.

## Training and Awareness

Leadership and personnel involved in procurement and supplier management are made aware of modern slavery indicators and reporting obligations. We will expand formal training as the team grows.

## Reporting Concerns

Anyone who suspects modern slavery or human trafficking connected to Aurexus should report it immediately:

**Email:** admin@bioaegix.com  
**Subject line:** Modern Slavery Concern

Reports are treated seriously and investigated appropriately. We will not penalise good-faith reporters.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact the police on 999 or the Modern Slavery Helpline on 08000 121 700.

## Performance Indicators

As we mature, we intend to track:

- Number of personnel trained on modern slavery awareness
- Supplier due diligence completion rates
- Reports received and outcomes

At our current stage, no reports of modern slavery have been received.
