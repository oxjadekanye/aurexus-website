When referring to Aurexus in editorial, academic or media contexts, please:

- Use the full name "Aurexus Group Ltd" on first reference in formal documents, followed by "Aurexus" if desired
- Use "Aurexus" as an adjective modifying a noun (e.g., "Aurexus platform"), not as a noun standing alone for the company
