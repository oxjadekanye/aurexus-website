## What This Website Actually Does

To keep this notice accurate, the current corporate website implementation is limited as follows:

- **Contact form:** Collects name, email address, organisation (optional), area of interest and message. Submissions are validated server-side and delivered to Aurexus by email (or via your email client where a mail-delivery service is not configured). A honeypot field is used for basic spam protection.
- **Theme preference:** Dark/light mode preference may be stored in your browser (local storage) so the site can remember your choice. This is treated as strictly necessary functional storage.
- **Cookie consent preference:** Your cookie choices are stored in your browser (local storage) so we can honour them. This is strictly necessary for compliance with your preferences.
- **Analytics:** The corporate website does **not** currently load third-party analytics, advertising pixels, CRM trackers or marketing tags.
- **Hosting:** The site is a static/server-rendered Next.js application. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical logs (such as IP addresses and request metadata) as part of operating the website securely. Those providers act as processors where they handle personal data on our behalf.

We do not currently operate website user accounts, payment processing, live chat widgets or embedded social media plugins on this corporate site.
