## Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules for using websites, platforms and digital services operated by Aurexus Group Ltd ("Aurexus", "we", "us" or "our"), including [https://www.aurexus-group.com](https://www.aurexus-group.com), [https://www.bioaegix.com](https://www.bioaegix.com) and related products (NPTTE PharmaNG, BeatIQ).

This AUP applies to all users, including visitors, registered users, clients, partners, contractors and administrators. It supplements our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms-of-use), [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms-and-conditions) and any applicable service agreements.

## Purpose

Aurexus builds responsible AI ecosystems for intelligent transformation. This policy exists to:

- Protect the security, integrity and availability of our systems
- Safeguard users, clients and third parties
- Ensure lawful, ethical and professional use of our technology
- Support compliance with UK GDPR, sector regulations and contractual obligations

## Permitted Use

You may use our services only for their intended purpose and in compliance with all applicable laws, contracts and policies. Permitted activities include:

- Accessing public website content for lawful information and enquiry purposes
- Using licensed platform features in accordance with your agreement and role
- Submitting accurate information through authorised channels
- Reporting security vulnerabilities through our [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](/legal/vulnerability-disclosure)

## Prohibited Conduct

You must not:

### Security and system integrity

- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to systems, accounts, data or networks
- Probe, scan or test vulnerabilities except through our authorised disclosure programme
- Introduce malware, viruses, ransomware or harmful code
- Circumvent authentication, access controls or rate limits
- Interfere with or disrupt services, servers or infrastructure

### Unlawful and harmful activity

- Use services for any unlawful purpose or to facilitate illegal activity
- Upload, transmit or store content that is defamatory, harassing, threatening, obscene or discriminatory
- Infringe intellectual property, privacy or other rights of third parties
- Process special category data without lawful authority and appropriate safeguards
- Use AI outputs to make unlawful automated decisions affecting individuals

### Misrepresentation and abuse

- Impersonate any person or entity, or misrepresent your affiliation with Aurexus
- Provide false or misleading information in registrations or communications
- Use services to send unsolicited commercial communications (spam)
- Create multiple accounts to evade restrictions or enforcement
- Scrape, harvest or systematically extract data without written permission

### Platform-specific restrictions

- Use healthcare platforms outside authorised clinical or operational contexts
- Override, disable or circumvent human oversight, audit logging or governance controls
- Use AI features to replace mandatory professional judgement where human accountability is required
- Share credentials or permit unauthorised access to licensed environments

## AI-Specific Requirements

Where our platforms incorporate artificial intelligence:

- AI outputs must be treated as decision-support unless expressly designated otherwise under contract
- You must not rely solely on AI outputs for safety-critical, clinical or legally significant decisions without appropriate human review
- You must not attempt to extract model weights, training data or proprietary algorithms
- You must not use our systems to develop competing models through systematic output harvesting
- You must report materially incorrect, harmful or biased outputs through appropriate channels

See our [Responsible AI Policy](/legal/responsible-ai) and [AI Transparency Statement](/legal/ai-transparency).

## Content Standards

User-generated content (including support tickets, feedback, uploads and communications) must:

- Be accurate where statements of fact are made
- Respect confidentiality obligations toward clients, patients and partners
- Not contain protected health information unless submitted through authorised secure channels with lawful basis
- Comply with professional standards applicable to your role

We may remove content or suspend access that violates this policy.

## Monitoring and Enforcement

We reserve the right to:

- Monitor use of our services for security, compliance and operational purposes
- Investigate suspected violations
- Suspend or terminate access without notice where necessary to protect systems or users
- Report unlawful activity to law enforcement or regulators
- Cooperate with legal process and regulatory enquiries

Monitoring is conducted proportionately and in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## Reporting Violations

To report suspected violations of this policy:

**Email:** admin@bioaegix.com  
**Subject line:** Acceptable Use Report

Include relevant details, timestamps and evidence where available. Do not include unnecessary personal data.

For security issues, use our [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](/legal/vulnerability-disclosure).

## Consequences of Violation

Violations may result in:

- Warning and requirement to cease prohibited activity
- Temporary or permanent suspension of access
- Termination of contractual relationships
- Legal action to recover losses and obtain injunctive relief
- Referral to regulatory or law enforcement authorities

## Changes

We may update this AUP as our services and regulatory environment evolve. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.
