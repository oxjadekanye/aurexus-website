## Overview

BioAegix is Aurexus Group Ltd's flagship Intelligent Healthcare Ecosystem. It is not simply a care management system. It is an AI-powered digital healthcare ecosystem designed to intelligently connect healthcare professionals, care providers, residents, families and healthcare stakeholders within one secure platform.

BioAegix is built as an enterprise healthcare ecosystem to improve quality of care, operational efficiency, compliance and clinical decision-making. It should not be understood as only a care-home application.

## Artificial intelligence in BioAegix

BioAegix treats artificial intelligence as an embedded organisational capability—not the centre of the platform. AI supports care planning, clinical recommendations, intelligence and reporting while authorised professionals retain judgement and accountability.
