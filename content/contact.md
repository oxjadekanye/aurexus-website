## Contact form

When submitting an enquiry, please include:

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Name** | Your full name |
| **Email** | A valid email address for our response |
| **Organisation** | Your company, institution or affiliation (if applicable) |
| **Enquiry type** | Partnership · Research collaboration · Enterprise discussion · Careers / talent network · General enquiry |
| **Message** | A brief description of your interest and how Aurexus may be able to help |

## What to expect

- **Partnership enquiries** — We will arrange an initial conversation to understand your organisation, objectives and how Aurexus capabilities may align.
- **Research collaboration** — We will discuss mutual research interests, governance expectations and potential collaboration structures.
- **Enterprise discussions** — We will explore your operational context and whether the Aurexus Method™ is appropriate for your transformation needs.
- **Careers / talent network** — We will acknowledge your interest and retain your details for future consideration, with your consent.

We do not use high-pressure sales tactics. Every conversation begins with understanding—not pitching.
